# Post meetings
We meet on the **fourth Tuesday of each month** at the Post hall. **Dinner is served at 5:00 p.m., and the meeting begins at 5:30 p.m.**
Our December meeting is usually canceled for the holidays. Please [contact the Post]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}) to confirm any schedule changes.
