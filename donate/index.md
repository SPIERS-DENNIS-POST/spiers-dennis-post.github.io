# Support Post 24
Spiers-Dennis Post No. 24 brings veterans together, remembers those who served, and provides a place for fellowship and community. Money, materials, and practical assistance all help sustain that work.
## Ways to give
To make a monetary contribution, <a href="{{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}">contact the Post</a> for current donation methods, instructions for checks or approved online giving, or to discuss a gift for a particular purpose.

Please confirm the check payee and mailing instructions before sending a contribution.
## Material donations
Durable, useful goods can help the Post just as much as money. We welcome offers of materials that store well and can serve practical needs, including:
- **Construction and grounds:** dimensional lumber, T-posts, engineer stakes, construction screws, nails, and other hardware.
- **Tools and equipment:** usable hand tools, power tools, accessories, and maintenance supplies.
- **Food and storage:** unopened canned goods, shelf-stable food, food-grade containers, and bulk food-storage supplies.
- **Computers:** working computers and useful accessories, with personal information removed.
- **Flags and military history:** flags in good condition and historic military service items.
- **Coffee and related supplies:** ground coffee, whole coffee beans, cups, mugs, and coffee filters.
- **By prior arrangement:** firewood, ethanol-free gasoline in approved containers, precious metals, and other materials needing special storage or handling.

Please arrange acceptance and delivery with the Post in advance. Do not leave donated items unattended; fuel and high-value gifts require suitable handling and secure receipt.

## Transparency
**You are entitled to know where our money goes.** Spiers-Dennis Post No. 24 is accountable to its members, donors, and community for how it receives and uses funds and donated property. We welcome questions about income, expenditures, Post projects, and how material contributions are used.
To request information about Post finances, available records, or the use of a contribution, <a href="{{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}">contact the Post</a>. You may also request a donation acknowledgment.
The 2014 IRS determination records **“Contribution Deductibility: Yes.”** This is a historical finding, not a verification of current standing or a determination about any particular gift. Current IRS information can be checked through the <a href="https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/search-for-tax-exempt-organizations">IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search</a>. Colorado registration details are available from the <a href="https://www.sos.state.co.us/biz/BusinessEntityCriteriaExt.do?resetTransTyp=Y">Colorado Secretary of State</a>.
These records document the Post's organizational status; they are not current financial statements or accounts of expenditures. Other approved records are listed in the <a href="{{ '/documents/' | relative_url }}">Public Documents directory</a>.
