Office terminology

The American Legion's 2026 Officer's Guide uses the title Finance Officer, while its suggested post bylaws also use Finance Officer/Treasurer.[2] This roster uses Finance Officer; the Post's adopted local governing documents have not yet been published here.

Contacting the Post

All inquiries should use the Post's public desk telephone or organizational email. Personal contact information for individual members or officers is never published.
