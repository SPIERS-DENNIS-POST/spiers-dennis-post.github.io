# Privacy
**Updated October 27, 2026**
This website does not use advertising, visitor accounts, contact forms, or analytics scripts. You can read its pages without signing in or providing personal information to the Post.
## Website hosting
The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it collects and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes. Read the [GitHub Pages privacy information](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages) and [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.
## Other websites
Links to The American Legion and other organizations lead to websites with their own privacy practices.
## Post contact information
We publish the Post's shared phone number and email address. We do not publish private contact information for individual members or officers.
