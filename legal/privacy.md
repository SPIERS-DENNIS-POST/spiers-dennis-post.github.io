# Privacy Policy
**Last reviewed:** October 27, 2026.
## Scope
This statement describes the current Spiers-Dennis Post informational website at [spiers-dennis-post.github.io](https://spiers-dennis-post.github.io/). It concerns use of this website, not the separate privacy practices of The American Legion, GitHub, or other organizations.
## Data collected by this website
The website's current source does not include a visitor account system, contact forms, first-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking scripts. The Post does not currently ask visitors to submit personal information through this website. If those features are introduced, this notice should be reviewed before they are enabled.
## Hosting and technical records
This website is hosted by **GitHub Pages**, a service operated by GitHub. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes when a GitHub Pages site is visited, including visits by people who are not signed in to GitHub.
For GitHub's own data handling, retention, security practices, and privacy rights information, read [GitHub's explanation of GitHub Pages data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages) and the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Those practices are controlled by GitHub, not by the Post website.
## External websites
Links to The American Legion, GitHub, or other outside websites lead to services with their own terms and privacy practices. Visitors should review those services' notices when appropriate.
## Information about individuals
Only the Post's shared desk telephone and official Post email are published as contact channels. Members' and officers' personal phone numbers, private email addresses, home addresses, and other personal contact information will not be published. This also applies to source documents, images, and metadata.
Published Post records, photographs, letters, and historical documents may identify people. Publication of personal or sensitive information should be reviewed for accuracy, appropriateness, and any necessary permissions before it is added to the repository.
## Changes and privacy questions
This page will be revised if the website begins collecting visitor submissions or using additional third-party services. For questions about this statement, contact the Post at [{{ site.data.post.contact.email }}](mailto:{{ site.data.post.contact.email }}).
