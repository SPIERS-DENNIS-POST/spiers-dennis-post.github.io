# Accessibility statement
**Last reviewed:** October 27, 2026.
## Accessibility objective
The website is being designed to be usable with a wide range of browsers, devices, keyboards, and assistive technologies. Its development target is the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) at Level AA.
This is a development objective, **not** a claim that the site has been independently audited, certified, or fully conforms to every WCAG success criterion.
## Current approach
- Content is delivered as ordinary HTML and remains available without JavaScript.
- Pages use headings, descriptive links, semantic document structure, and a keyboard-accessible skip link.
- Layouts adapt to smaller screens, and nonessential movement respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Source files are maintained as text; important published documents should be accompanied by accessible HTML versions whenever practicable.
## Known limitations
The website is under construction, and several content sections have not yet been populated. Future scanned documents, photographs, or third-party resources may have accessibility limitations. These should be assessed when added, with accessible alternatives provided where practicable.
## Reporting barriers
If a page, document, or feature is difficult to use, it would be helpful to identify the affected page and describe the problem. Accessibility concerns can be sent to the Post's [public email](mailto:{{ site.data.post.contact.email }}) or reported using its [desk telephone](tel:+1{{ site.data.post.contact.phone | remove: '-' }}). No individual's personal contact information is published.
## Accessibility reference
For the technical standard, consult the [W3C Web Accessibility Initiative's WCAG overview](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/).
