# Copyright & reuse
**Last reviewed:** October 27, 2026.
## Reuse of factual information
The website is intended to make Post-specific factual information easy to discover, cite, and verify. Visitors may link to its public pages and cite factual statements with attribution to the Post and, where available, the underlying records.
Providing public access to a page or source document does **not**, by itself, grant a license to reproduce its original text, photographs, artwork, audio, or other protected expression.
## Website and Post materials
Rights to original website content, Post publications, records, photographs, and other material depend on authorship, contracts, permissions, and applicable law. Do not assume every item has the same copyright owner or reuse terms.
Where a particular item has an explicit license or permission statement, that item's notice governs its permitted reuse. The public [source repository](https://github.com/SPIERS-DENNIS-POST/spiers-dennis-post.github.io) is available for inspection; public source availability does not automatically license reuse of its contents.

Some materials may have been created by people or organizations other than the Post. Their inclusion in an archive does not imply that the Post owns their rights or can authorize third-party reproduction. Credit, date, provenance, and known rights information should accompany archival items where available.

The American Legion's name, emblem, and brand marks are protected and subject to the national organization's rules. This website does not grant permission to reproduce or use those marks. Consult [The American Legion's official emblem and brand-mark guidance](https://www.legion.org/about/organization/the-emblem/emblem-and-brand-mark-download).
