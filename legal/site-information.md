## Scope and authority
This website is being developed as a public reference for **Post-specific** information, including meeting details, governance documents, building information, history, news, and commander's letters.
The site does not speak for American Legion National Headquarters or substitute for national rules. Matters such as national membership eligibility and Legion-wide programs are referred to [official American Legion sources]({{ '/legion/' | relative_url }}).
The Post's [Constitution and Bylaws]({{ '/governance/' | relative_url }}) govern where applicable; this website information page does not create or amend them.
## Accuracy and publication status
The website is still being populated. Unverified or unavailable facts are not presented as settled information. Dates, meeting arrangements, officeholders, facilities, and policies may change.
Older news items and commander's letters are historical publications and should be interpreted according to their original dates. A historical statement should not automatically be treated as current policy.
Information is provided for public reference in good faith. For important decisions, check the applicable original records and any more recent authorized Post communication.
## Corrections and records
See [Sources & Corrections]({{ '/sources/' | relative_url }}) for the website's source standards. For corrections, use the Post's [public email](mailto:{{ site.data.post.contact.email }}). Do not send sensitive member information or private contact details.
## Legal review
These website statements are not legal advice and do not replace any applicable legal obligation. Final policy approval and the Post's formal publishing authority remain matters for authorized Post leadership.
