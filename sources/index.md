# Sources & corrections
Post-specific assertions should cite official records, approved publications, or other clearly identified sources where appropriate.
Each citation should identify the source document or web page, relevant section, and publication or effective date if known.
A public corrections contact will be published here once authorized. Until then, do not use repository issues to disclose personal or confidential information.
